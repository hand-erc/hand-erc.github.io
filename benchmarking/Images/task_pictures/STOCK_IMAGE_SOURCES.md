The final photograph must contain ONLY the existing wooden table and the existing human hand grasping the thin silver washer above the table. Extend the clean wooden tabletop naturally into the cleared background, with consistent perspective, grain, lighting and shallow depth of field. Preserve the original hand pose, skin texture, fingertip placement, washer shape and scale, original composition and camera angle. Do not replace or reshape the hand or washer; do not add any props, text, borders or watermark. Photorealistic background cleanup, not a new illustration.
